QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you invite
the whole family.

The short version: your memories stay yours, sharing stays intentional, and nobody needs to be the family tech support.

Still curious? Get in touch →
Are my family's files private?

Yes. Every Tether space is private by default, and only people you invite can see or contribute to it. Your memories are never placed in a public feed.

What kinds of files can I keep?

Photos, videos, PDFs, scanned artwork, school documents, recipes, letters, and other family files can all live together in the same organized space.

Will grandparents find it easy to use?

That is a core part of Tether's design. Invited family members open one clean feed with clear actions and no camera-roll clutter or buried sharing menus.

Can I export everything later?

Yes. Every plan includes portable exports, so your original files and the family context around them are never locked into Tether.

Can different family members add memories?

Yes. Members you invite can contribute their own photos, documents, videos, and notes to the spaces they share with you.

Is Tether available on Android?

Tether is launching on iPhone first. Android access is planned, and the website will be updated as soon as it is ready.
